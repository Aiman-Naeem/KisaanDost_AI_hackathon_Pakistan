### KisaanDost
- Definition：Internal codename of the Expo-based farmer assistant mobile app being built for Pakistan's first AI Hackathon by Alkhidmat Foundation in collaboration with Alibaba Cloud. The name combines Urdu/Hindi "kisaan" (farmer) and "dost" (friend).
